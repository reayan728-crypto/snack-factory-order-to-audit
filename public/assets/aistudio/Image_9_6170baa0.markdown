## Brand & Style

This design system targets manufacturing plant supervisors, production dispatchers, and line engineers operating in high-tempo, data-dense industrial environments. The interface prioritizes zero-latency visual parsing, absolute legibility from arm's-length or wall-mounted touch displays, and rock-solid operational confidence. 

The aesthetic is **Utilitarian High-Density Precision**:
- Strips decorative ornamentation in favor of crisp visual hierarchy, calibrated contrast, and unambiguous semantic signaling.
- Bridges modern enterprise software with industrial instrumentation interfacesâ€”reminiscent of aerospace consoles and precision calibration tools.
- Visual weight is reserved strictly for operational status, line variances, alerts, and active throughput metrics.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for prolonged shift use, reducing eye fatigue while offering high-contrast clarity:

- **Primary (`#1E293B`)**: Deep Industrial Slate. Used for primary chrome, top-level navigation headers, key interactive commands, and authoritative typography.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: High-Vis Electric Indigo. Used for active operational targets, focused states, primary action triggers, batch routing selections, and interactive system nodes.
- **Tertiary / Alert Red (`#DC2626`)**: Critical Variance Red. Reserved strictly for out-of-spec tolerances, emergency stops, downtime indicators, and catastrophic failure warnings. Supported by soft tinted background states (`#FEF2F2`) and live highlight rings (`#EF4444`).
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: Background baseline uses a low-glare slate neutral (`#F8FAFC`), structured against crisp containment borders (`#E2E8F0`) and muted foreground text (`#64748B`). Dark mode shifts the canvas to `#0F172A` with `#1E293B` containers and `#334155` structural dividing rules.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces strict role separation between operational status, contextual metadata, and telemetry data:

- **Headers (`Geist`)**: Clean, geometric, and authoritative. Used for workstation designations, line numbers, and module headers.
- **Interface & Text (`Inter`)**: Engineered for small-screen density, high contrast, and neutral legibility across long shifts.
- **Telemetry & Numbers (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Mandated for all counts, timestamps, serial identifiers, tolerances, and sensor readings. Tabular figures ensure uniform alignment across high-frequency vertical columns.
